07 · Wholesale 101

Chapter seven · Wholesale 101

Learn the game before you change it.

Six short explainers on how wholesale actually works. No jargon.

01 · IMO vs. wholesale

An IMO sits between you and the carrier and keeps the spread. Wholesale means you keep it.

The IMO holds the contract, sets your level, and keeps the difference between what the carrier pays and what you see. Agora Wholesale starts from direct contracts, so that layer is not there.

02 · Contract levels

A level is the share of first-year premium the carrier pays at your position.

A "120" means 120 percent of target premium in year one. The higher you sit, the more of every policy you keep. Levels differ by carrier and product line, and renewals matter as much as year one.

03 · White-label

Your brand on the screen, our engine underneath.

The dashboard, promotions, onboarding and reporting with your logo, colors and domain. Your producers never see Agora. Builds are scoped per agency, and the shared portal works without one.

04 · Lead pricing

Agora sets the price. You see it before you buy.

Leads are tagged by source, vertical and age and priced at cost-plus. Because the lead store and reporting share one system, outcomes feed straight back into your numbers.

05 · Marketplace

We do not build every tool. We negotiate the price of the best ones.

Quoting engines, rate watchers, estate and tax partners at partner pricing. Where a partner shares revenue on completed work, that share flows to the agency that wrote the client.

06 · The reporting layer

Think Pentagon, not Army. Agora sees the whole board without running your agency.

Production, renewals and who owes what, across carriers, in one place. Partner agencies plug into the same reporting, and carrier feeds connect one carrier at a time.

09 · Straight answers

Chapter nine · Straight answers

Before we talk, the questions principals ask first.

Who owns the book of business?

Your agency does. The book you write through Agora is yours, and so are your producers and your name.

Will you release us if we leave?

Yes. If you decide to move on, ask and we release you. No hostage contracts.

Which carriers are available?

North American, F&G, National Life Group, American-Amicable, Athene, Foresters Financial, Royal Neighbors of America and Ameritas, all at direct level. The carrier grid and levels are shared privately after review.

Who is Agora Wholesale for?

Agencies that already produce: principals with producers writing business every week who want direct-level contracts, their own technology and a lead supply they control.

Is this a hierarchy we have to climb?

No. Agora is not a ladder. Beyond the contracts, Agora is the visibility and resource layer: reporting, technology, leads and tools in one place, used only if you want them.

What does it cost?

Contract access has no platform fee. White-labeled technology is a paid build. Leads and marketplace tools are priced per item and shown before you buy. Training is available and never required.

What happens after I request a review?

A principal reads every request. If your agency fits, we schedule a private call, walk through the carrier grid, and once approved your portal login arrives by email.
